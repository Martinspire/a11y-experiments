A11y - modern accessibility

A11y is short for accessibility. It is a term used to describe the design of products, devices, services, or environments for people with disabilities. The concept of a11y is to make things usable for everyone, regardless of their abilities.

Accessibility for the web is handled by the W3C, the World Wide Web Consortium. They have a set of guidelines called WCAG, the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines. These guidelines are designed to make web content more accessible to people with disabilities.

A11y is a big topic and it is important to understand the basics. We have a few sections that cover different aspects of accessibility.

  • WCAG - the guidelines and rules that have become law in a lot of countries like in the EU and US.
  • Aria - the implementation for HTML, CSS, Javascript
  • Posters - to share awareness
