What does hearing loss mean

When somebody says they have hearing loss, they mean they can't hear all the sounds at the same volume and frequency that people normally do.

The way to visualise hearing loss is with an Audiogram, which is also set up with hearing tests to indicate the loss and is the baseline for setting up hearing aids. Alternatively you can rudimentary test your hearing with the following test: https://www.checkhearing.org/index.php, which has a few tests available to check your hearing (mainly if you use the hamburger-button on the top-right of the page to see what they have).

An audiogram is a graph with frequency horizontally and loudness vertically. There will be various dots on the chart and if you draw a line between them, you get the average loss of loudness on that frequency (though it might not be that black and white for every frequency between those lines, but more detail would require a lot more time and work to figure it out).
