Subtitles and captions matter

Subtitles are a great way to make your content more accessible to a larger audience, even those from your own country. And it's not just about the hearing impaired. People with dyslexia, people that are learning a new language, or people that are in a loud environment can benefit from subtitles

Watch the video below from the lady doing ASL. See if you can figure out what she is saying.

Of course the key to understanding her, is to enable captions. If you haven't done already, enable them and figure out what the video is about.
