Experience colorblindness

Various people experience color blindness. There's a wide range of variants, but the most common ones are listed below. This page allows you to browse various pictures of color blindness comparisons. This obviously works best when you aren't color blind, but if 2 images look the same, you should probably contact your doctor.

How common

  • Deuteranopia or deuteranomoly: 5% of all males.
  • Protanopia or protanomaly: 2.5% of all males.
  • Tritanopia: Less than 0.3% of women and men.
